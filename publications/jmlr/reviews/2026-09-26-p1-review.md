Original finding (optional, nonblocking), foundations.tex:86–91: “On every positive-probability pair event” could refer to unconditional probability, although the sampling probabilities depend on the history. The intended support is {sigma_k(i)>0}. A pair may have positive unconditional probability while having zero conditional probability on some histories. The surrounding history definitions and explicit zero-probability disclaimer make the intended meaning recoverable, so this does not block the gate. Suggested resolution: say that the identities hold almost surely on {sigma_k(i)>0}, with no restriction on conditional versions outside that set.

### P1-v1-N-A-02: deterministic coefficient interpretation
