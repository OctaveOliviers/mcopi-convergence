# Exposition session format

The writer maintains this record under the paper's `reviews/` directory and links it from `STATUS.md`. It contains private assessment material: never supply the whole populated file to an exposition checker. Save the unassisted response before the dialogue.

## Assignment and version

Record session/result IDs, exact manuscript/dependency version, clearance review IDs for correctness and notation, actual reader model/effort when exposed, agreed prerequisites, supplied text and scope. Record any exposure to excluded material. A preliminary diagnostic without clearance is labelled preliminary.

## Author assessment prepared before reader answers

Record the essential mechanism, why the main steps and assumptions matter, important inferences to probe, and informative simple transfer questions. Identify the checked manuscript support for these expectations. Accept alternative mathematically valid accounts. This section and the questions' expected answers remain private from the reader.

## Reader's independent response

Save the reader's actual initial account of the claim, mechanism, dependency structure and difficult steps. Record reading stops with locations, attempted interpretations and whether later text resolved them. Mark untested aspects and uncertainty; do not replace the response with an improved author paraphrase.

## Socratic exchange

Save the questions and answers in order. Mark every hint, correction or explanation as assistance, including when the reader requests it. Distinguish responses supported by the manuscript from responses learned during the exchange. The reader may question the writer's premise.

## Assessment and disposition

For each substantive difficulty, record its classification: prerequisite gap, exposition gap, notation burden, reader error, or suspected mathematical gap. Record the evidence and disposition in the shared finding scheme from [review-record.md](../write-and-review-paper/review-record.md). Mathematical disputes go to correctness.

Use `pass`, `revise`, or `inconclusive` for exposition, with reasons and tested scope. A pass requires accurate unassisted understanding of the mechanism and essential inferences, supported by the actual explain-back and answers. Any success relying on tutoring is diagnostic only. No number of successful coached answers converts it to an independent pass.

Record proposed deletion/restructuring/clarification and why it helps; “no useful cuts” is valid. For a revised manuscript, link new correctness/notation evidence as required and the fresh reader's session. For an unchanged retest, state the specific reason and retain contrary evidence; do not select only favorable runs. Record the writer's assessment separately from the reader's self-report.
